# Lil Betito

A small Android game that floats over your screen. Lil Betito never stops moving. Steer him with one or two fingers, crush the bricks for points, and when you hit the target the door in the ceiling opens. Get through before the rising floor squishes him.

## How to play
- One finger pulls him toward it. With two fingers, the first is the anchor and the second sets the direction.
- Crush bricks worth 10, 25 or 50. Every quarter of the target drops the floor and buys you time.
- Reach the target and the door opens. Fly through it to reach the next level.
- Escaping pays 100, 300 or 600 points, 1000 at the last moment, plus a speed bonus. Later rooms multiply it.
- Casino sounds get bigger as your combo grows.

## Install
Open the Actions tab, tap the latest Build APK run, download the screen-skater-apk artifact, and install the APK. Allow Display over other apps when asked.
