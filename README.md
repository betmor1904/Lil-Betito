# Lil Betito

A small Android game that floats over your screen. Launch a tiny turtle, let him ricochet around the room, and use your 3 nudges to steer him through the gap in the ceiling before the rising floor squishes him. Every room is harder than the last.

## How to play
- Drag Lil Betito back and let go to launch him. The farther you drag, the faster he goes.
- He bounces between the floor and ceiling at constant speed. Every bump makes noise, and obstacles are the loudest.
- Tap anywhere to nudge him (3 per level). He gets pushed away from your tap.
- Get through the gap in the ceiling to reach the next room.
- Score: 100, 300 or 600 points, and 1000 at the last moment. Rooms 6-10 are 2x, 11-20 are 3x, 21+ are 5x.

## Install
Open the Actions tab, tap the latest Build APK run, download the screen-skater-apk artifact, and install the APK. Allow Display over other apps when asked.
