**Full description** (max 4000):

Betito the turtle is the ball. The ocean is the course.

Pull back, let go, and slingshot Betito the turtle through flooded mazes to the glowing hole, in as few shots as you can. It's golf, but underwater, with currents.

🌊 RIDE THE CURRENTS
Blue currents are boost lanes. Green ones push you up and down. Red riptides shove you backward. Chain two, three, four currents in a single shot for NICE!!, DEADLY COMBO!! and KING OF THE SEA!!

🎯 SHOOT THE TARGETS
Hit a buoy and its current flips direction: turn a riptide into a rocket. Hit a gold lock to blast a shortcut through the wall. Pick off pink stars for bonus points. Hit two or three in one shot for DOUBLE TAP!! and TRIPLE THREAT!!

⛳ BEAT PAR
Every hole has a par. Go under it for a BIRDIE, EAGLE, or the legendary HOLE IN ONE. Bank shots off the rocks, whip around corners on kelp slides, and let the hole suck you in.

🎰 SPIN THE REELS
Apples and big combos fill the slot reels. Three of a kind pays out big. 777 = JACKPOT.

🐙 MEET OCTO
Octo the octopus is Betito's caddie. Every time something new shows up, he stops and explains it, so you're never lost.

🦈 WATCH FOR SHARKS
They won't bite, but they'll bump your perfect shot right off course.
