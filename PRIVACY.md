# Turtle Shot – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Turtle Shot is a single-player game for Android. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Turtle Shot does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information.

- No account or sign-in.
- No internet connection is used by the game.
- No analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves a few things **only on your own device** so it can work:

- Your progress (current hole and score) and best score.
- Your settings (sound, Octo's tips).
- If you choose a photo for "Beto's photo", a small copy of that one picture is saved inside the app's private storage. It is never uploaded or shared. You can remove it any time in Settings, or by uninstalling the game.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of this.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If the game ever adds ads or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will list exactly what is collected and by whom.
